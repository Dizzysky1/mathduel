How it works
  • Both players get the same question at the same time. First correct answer scores. A wrong answer locks you out of that question.
  • Type numbers as integers, decimals or fractions (3/4). Multiple-choice questions accept keys 1-4.
  • Ties go to sudden death. Online play is peer-to-peer over WebRTC; nothing is stored on any server.
